What is this?

A free community for women building businesses, whatever the stage, industry or size.

We meet monthly, online and in person whenever we can.

Each meeting runs like a board meeting. Everyone shares a business update, their highlights and challenges, and any asks for the group.

In between, we are there for each other. For big things: introductions, honest feedback or being the sounding board for a tough call. And small things, like engaging with your latest LinkedIn post.

What should I expect?

Each month, come ready to share an update on what you're working on. We'll give you a simple structure to follow.

It keeps us accountable, helps us learn from each other and means we always have support.

Between meetings, we stay connected, so you can ask for help, big or small, anytime.

Everything shared is confidential. When you join, you commit to no ego and no judgement, and to always support the others in the group.

Is this for me?

If you identify as a woman and you're building your own business, this space is for you.

Any stage, size or type: tech, NGO, physical goods, agency and more.

If you can commit to our values (no ego, no judgement, 100% support), we'd love you to join us. It's free.
